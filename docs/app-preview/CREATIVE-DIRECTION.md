# A clearer plan, with a friendly guide

September 9, 2026. This pass responds to the request for larger typography and a reason to care before the how-to.

## What the references contribute

- [Sandwich's Slack campaign](https://sandwich.co/clients/slack/): the creator describes using its team's initial skepticism and ordinary working interactions to explain the benefit. Application: begin with the recognizable “what now?” moment, then show a plan, risk and help. Do not fabricate customer testimony or borrow their claimed results.
- [Apple's Mac presentation](https://www.apple.com/mac/): benefit headlines accompany the actual product. Application: one memorable promise, quiet supporting layout, and the interactive product as demonstration. This is a design interpretation, not evidence of conversion lift.
- [Duolingo's character design](https://blog.duolingo.com/building-character/): characters express the product's personality and connect to everyday situations. Application: give the existing Pip a clear role as a friendly guide without introducing a new mascot or decorative cast.

These are selected creative references, not an objective ranking of the strongest ads. No performance uplift is claimed for the redesign.

## Compact design plan

- Color: forest #0b5d3f, ink #17251e, paper #fafbf9, mint #ddeade, neutral #536259. Red #cd343c stays tied to stop/risk illustrations; no added gold marketing decoration.
- Type: the app's familiar system sans, rounded system display for the headline. Display 68–76px desktop / 42–48px mobile; page body 20–22px / 18px; walkthrough explanations 22px / 18px; controls and supporting copy 16px. Expanded app text is unscaled.
- Layout: a large, left-aligned human question beside the compact phone. Three visual benefit stories follow; the complete feature directory follows the why. Desktop walkthrough uses the app beside its explanation; narrow screens keep an unscaled app and readable explanation with independent scrolling when height is limited.
- Principle: demonstrate a clearer decision process and approachable help, not improved investment returns. Friendly is expressed through voice, readable type and Pip's useful role.

## Review before building

Rejected a generic “less stress, more success” headline: it could describe any finance product and imply an outcome we cannot substantiate. “Turn ‘what now?’ into a plan” describes the specific research-to-planning gap the app addresses. Keep the existing product colors, app identity and native layouts; concentrate the creative change in the opening line and the benefit demonstrations.

The tour's first five chapters establish the problem, plan, risk, friendly help and community before the detailed feature tour. Preserve all 16 chapters and full app navigation. All examples remain clearly simulated.
