A few friendly answers.

Can I try both the iOS and macOS apps?

Yes. Use the iOS / macOS switch to explore the iPhone layout or the Mac workspace. These are interactive browser recreations using simulated data. The native apps connect to the actual services.

Can I pause the film and use the app myself?

Yes. Choose “Pause and explore” to open the app at that moment. “Resume film” takes you back to the same time. You can also open any screen.

Are these live prices or real member conversations?

No. Every market example, event and conversation in the preview is simulated. Your demo posts, plans and alerts stay inside this preview and reset when you reload.

Can I trade through this preview?

No. The preview demonstrates research and planning tools. It does not connect to a brokerage, send alerts or place orders.

Can I switch devices during the preview?

Yes. Your current screen, sample plans and demo changes stay with you when you switch between the iOS and macOS previews. On a small screen, the Mac sidebar opens with its menu button. The native Mac app requires macOS 14 or later.
