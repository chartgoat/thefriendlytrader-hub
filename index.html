A few friendly answers.

Is this the actual iPhone app?

This is an interactive browser recreation of the app’s main screens and workflows. It uses simulated data so you can explore without an account. The iPhone app connects to the actual services.

Can I pause the tour and use it myself?

Yes. Tap a control or pause the tour to take over. Resume returns you to the saved tour position. You can also jump directly to any screen.

Are these live prices or real member conversations?

No. Every market example, event and conversation in the preview is simulated. Your demo posts, plans and alerts stay inside this preview and reset when you reload.

Can I trade through this preview?

No. The preview demonstrates research and planning tools. It does not connect to a brokerage, send alerts or place orders.

What if I don’t have an iPhone?

You can explore the complete browser preview on your phone, tablet or computer. Existing web tools remain available through the links above.
