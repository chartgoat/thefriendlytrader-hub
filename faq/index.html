A few friendly answers.

The basics

What is Friendly Trader?

Trading tools that explain themselves in plain English, with Pip to answer your questions. One membership opens them all.

What do I get?

A+ Scalps, MACD, Patterns, Screener, Stop Finder, Friendly Options and Trade Tracker. Plus community chat, the Tools live room and alerts from the tools.

Do I need experience?

No. Everything is built so a beginner can follow it.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are educational tools. You make your own decisions, and trading involves risk.

Is there an iPhone app?

It’s coming soon to the App Store. Until then, use the web app on any device.

I saw this in one of Josh’s videos. What is it?

These are the tools from the team behind Stocks With Josh.

Getting in

How do I join?

Check out on Whop, then open the web app and choose Sign in with Whop.

Do I need a login for each tool?

No. Your Whop account is the login for every tool.

Will I have to sign anything?

Yes. The first time you sign in, you read the member agreement and sign by typing your name.

A tool says I’m not a member. What now?

Sign in with the same Whop account you joined with. Still stuck? Email support.

I’m already a Stocks With Josh member. Do I get the tools?

Most paid plans include them, and Trader Tier includes every tool. Sign in with your Whop account to see what’s open.

Cost and billing

How much does it cost?

Friendly Tools is $79.99 a month. Whop adds a 5% fee at checkout, so checkout shows $83.99.

What does Trader Tier add?

Live trading with Josh and every live room, on top of every tool. It starts at $49 a week, before Whop’s fee.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in your Whop account. You keep access through the time you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are final unless checkout or the law says otherwise. The member agreement has the details.

How do I get help?

Email support@stockswithjosh.com or ask in the community.
